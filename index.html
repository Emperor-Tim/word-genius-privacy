Word Genius Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Word Genius: Riddles & Puzzles is made by Timothy Mironenko. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. It doesn't need an account, it never asks for your name or email, and I don't sell your information.

Information kept on your device

Your progress through the packs, your hints, the days you've played, your settings and a record of what you've bought are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them, and Reset Progress in Settings clears your progress.

Advertising

Word Genius shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner, an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, and ads you can choose to watch for a free hint. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location
  • how you interact with the ads
  • your device's advertising identifier. On iPhone and iPad this is only shared if you allow tracking when iOS asks

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

Word Genius uses Unity Analytics to understand how the game is played, so I can make the puzzles and hints better. It records things like which puzzles are started and solved, how long they take, when hints are used or earned, when an ad is shown, when the Store is opened and what you buy there. Unity also collects information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language, your approximate location from your IP address, and a random identifier that the app creates for this installation. Everything Unity Analytics records is linked to that identifier, so it can tell what happens on the same device over time, but the identifier isn't connected to your name, your Apple Account or your Google Account.

Where the law requires your consent, analytics is only switched on if you agree to it in the privacy message, and nothing is sent until you've answered. Unity handles this information under its own privacy policy, at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Your choices

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are shown or any analytics is sent, the app asks for your consent using Google's privacy message. You can change your choices at any time from Settings in the app, under Privacy, then Privacy Options.
  • United States: if you live in a state with a privacy law that covers advertising, such as California, you can opt out of your information being sold or shared for personalised advertising from Settings in the app, under Privacy, then Privacy Options. You may also have the right to ask what information about you has been collected, or to ask for it to be deleted. I don't hold any information that identifies you, so for advertising those requests go to Google, and for analytics to Unity, through their privacy policies.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: iOS asks whether Word Genius can track you. You can change your answer at any time in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, but they're less personalised.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings, under Google, then Ads, or Privacy, then Ads, depending on your device.
  • Fewer ads: any purchase in the Store removes the banner and full-screen ads. Ads you choose to watch for a free hint are still offered, and the advertising service still runs so they can be.

Purchases

Apple and Google Play process purchases of puzzle packs, the Ultimate Upgrade and hints. I only receive confirmation of what you've bought, never your payment details or account information. Restore Purchases asks the store for the packs and upgrades you already own.

Ratings

Now and then the app may ask you to rate it, using Apple's or Google Play's own rating prompt. Anything you write there goes to the store, not to me.

Children

Word Genius isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
